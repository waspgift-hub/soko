FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is listing free?

Yes — listing products is completely free. You only pay when you choose to boost a listing.

How are payments protected?

Every payment is held safely in escrow until you confirm you have received your product. Then funds are released to the seller.

Can I talk to sellers?

Yes — real-time chat is available in the app before any purchase.

How does delivery work?

Nationwide delivery via bus and courier services. You will see the cost before you pay.
