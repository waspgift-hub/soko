FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Is listing a product really free?

Yes. Creating a listing costs nothing. You only pay when you choose to boost it for visibility.

How are my payments protected?

Payments go through a payment processor and sit in escrow until you confirm you received your order. If something goes wrong, you open a dispute and the team mediates.

Can I talk to a seller before buying?

Yes — every listing has an in-app chat. Ask questions, share photos and agree on price or delivery before you commit.

How does delivery work?

Sellers arrange nationwide delivery, and you track the handover in the app. You confirm the order after receiving it — that's when funds are released.
