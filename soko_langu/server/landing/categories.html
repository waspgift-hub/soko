Kategoria za Soko Vibe

Bidhaa kwenye Soko Vibe zimepangwa katika kategoria mbalimbali — vinjari unachotafuta kwenye app.

Soko Vibe ni marketplace ya Tanzania inayounganisha wanunuzi na wauzaji. Bidhaa zinatolewa katika kategoria zifuatazo:

Electronics

Vifaa vya kielektroniki — Phones & Tablets, Computers & Laptops, TV & Audio, Accessories. Binunue na kuuza vifaa vya umeme mpya na vya mitumba nchini Tanzania.

Fashion

Mitindo — Men's Clothing, Women's Clothing, Shoes, Bags & Luggage, Jewelry. Nguo, viatu na vifaa vya mtindo kwa wanunuzi na wauzaji Tanzania.

Home & Garden

Nyumba na bustani — Furniture, Kitchen & Dining, Home Decor, Garden & Outdoor, DIY Tools. Vifaa na fanicha za nyumbani.

Automotive

Magari — All Cars, Car Parts, Car Accessories, Tools & Equipment, Motorcycles. Magari, vipuri na vifaa vya magari.

Health & Beauty

Afya na urembo — Skincare, Hair Care, Makeup, Cosmetics, Supplements. Bidhaa za utunzaji wa ngozi, nywele na urembo.

Sports & Entertainment

Michezo na burudani — Fitness, Outdoor Sports, Games & Toys, Books & Media. Vifaa vya michezo na burudani.

Business & Industrial

Biashara na viwanda — Machinery, Construction, Agriculture, Office Supplies. Vifaa vya biashara, ujenzi na kilimo.

Food & Beverages

Chakula na vinywaji — Groceries, Snacks & Sweets, Beverages, Spices & Herbs. Bidhaa za chakula na vinywaji.

Maternal & Kids

Wamama na watoto — Baby Care, Kids Fashion, Kids Toys. Bidhaa za watoto na mama wajawazito.

Services

Huduma — Home Services, Repair & Maintenance, Education & Training. Huduma mbalimbali kwa nyumba na biashara.

Others

Nyingine — Other, Miscellaneous, Uncategorized. Bidhaa na huduma zisizoainishwa.

Vinjari kategoria hizi na utafute bidhaa mpya na za mitumba kwenye app ya Soko Vibe. Huduma zote — malipo ya escrow, mazungumzo na usafirishaji nchi nzima — zinapatikana pale kwenye app. Soma jinsi inavyofanya kazi, angalia ada za Soko Vibe au usome kuhusu escrow na usalama wa malipo.
