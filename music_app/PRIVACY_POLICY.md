# SokoVibe Music Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 October 2026

SokoVibe Music is a music discovery and playback application provided by SokoVibe. This policy describes the current version of the app. It must be reviewed whenever new account, advertising, analytics, payment, upload, or personalization features are introduced.

## Information handled by the app

- **Music searches and requests:** When you search for music, request recommendations, or request AI-generated original lyrics, the app sends the query or prompt to the SokoVibe Music Cloudflare Worker so it can provide the requested service.
- **Technical and security data:** Cloudflare receives network information such as your IP address and request metadata to deliver the service, prevent abuse, and operate its security and diagnostic logs. The app does not itself ask you to provide your precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone.
- **Music provider:** Search and track requests are processed by Jamendo through the SokoVibe backend. When you play a track, your device may connect to a music stream or artwork host supplied by the provider. Those services may process technical data under their own privacy policies.
- **AI lyrics:** If you use the lyrics-generation feature, the theme, genre, and language you enter are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate original lyrics. Do not include passwords or other sensitive personal information in a prompt.
- **On-device library and settings:** Theme, accent colour, reminder preferences, imported local audio references, and offline files are stored on your device or in the app's private storage. Imported audio is not uploaded by the current app version.
- **Accounts and payments:** The current app version does not provide user account creation, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or advertising SDK functionality.

## How information is used

Information is used to return music search results, resolve available track metadata and streams, generate requested original lyrics, save your preferences, maintain your local offline library, and protect the service from abuse. The app does not sell personal information.

## Sharing and service providers

The app uses Cloudflare for backend hosting, security, and (when requested) AI-generated lyrics, and Jamendo for music catalogue metadata and streams. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and under their own terms and privacy policies. The app does not intentionally share your local music files with these providers.

## Storage and retention

App preferences and imported local audio remain on your device until you remove them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Offline files saved by the app are kept in its private storage until removed or the app is uninstalled. Cloudflare may retain service and security logs according to the configuration and retention controls of the SokoVibe Cloudflare account. Provider-side retention is governed by the relevant provider's policies.

## Your choices

You can stop using online music and lyrics features by disconnecting from the internet, disable notifications through the app or Android settings, and remove imported/offline tracks from the app's offline library. You can clear app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored settings and app-private files.

## Security

SokoVibe uses HTTPS for backend requests and accepts only HTTPS track URLs for streaming and permitted downloads. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so do not submit sensitive information in search queries or lyrics prompts.

## Children's privacy

The current app version does not create user accounts or intentionally request personal information from children. Music catalogues and streams are provided by third parties; parents and guardians should supervise children's use and review the content and provider policies.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date above will be changed when a new version is published.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact **support@sokovibe.co.tz**.
