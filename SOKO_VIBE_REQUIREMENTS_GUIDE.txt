SOKO VIBE - MWONGOZO WA MAHITAJI (2026)
=======================================
TXT HII NI GUIDELINE - Fungua, pitia, rekebisha kabla ya kukamilisha.
Usifute mpaka mambo yote yamefanyika.

=====================================================================
[1] FLASH SALE
=====================================================================
1.1 Banner ya flash sale ionekane MARA MOJA baada ya kutengenezwa (ndani ya ms app iwe updated).
1.2 Picha ya bidhaa ifanye 75% ya banner ya flash sale (sio nusu, sio sehemu ndogo - 75%).
1.3 Flash sale ikitengenezwa -> push notification (OneSignal) + heads-up (local notification)
    kwa wateja: "fulani amepunguza bei ya bidhaa fulani".
1.4 Kwenye ku-create flash sale: mtumiaji akibonyeza "Watch Ad" -> ads ionekane MARA MOJA.

=====================================================================
[2] MALIPO / PAYMENT BANNER
=====================================================================
2.1 Banner isiwe "loading forever":
    - ClickPesa akisema failed -> banner iwe FAILED mara moja.
    - Baada ya failed, vitu vingine vitokee (error details / retry).
2.2 Banner yoyote ya malipo ikitokea -> mtumiaji ASIWEZE kufanya kitu kingine
    mpaka malipo yakamilike AU yawe failed (modal blocking).
    - Lakini kuwe na safety timeout (dakika 1) - banner isifunge app milele.
2.3 Shenghe (confetti) isijirudie mara mbili.
2.4 Success -> shenghe moja + risiti inaweza kutoka.

=====================================================================
[3] FLOW YA ORDER (ESCROW) - muhimu sana
=====================================================================
3.1 Buyer anaweka address -> anatuma order.
3.2 Order inafika kwa seller.
3.3 Seller anaona: buyer amechagua bidhaa GANI na anakaa WAPI
    (region/district/street/kijiji).
3.4 Seller anaweka bei ya shipping fee.
3.5 Buyer anapata taarifa -> analipa.
3.6 Malipo yanafanikiwa -> hela inaingia account ya ClickPesa, HAItolewi
    mpaka muuzaji atume mzigo (ESCROW).
3.7 Baada ya malipo kuwekwa escrow -> BUYER anajaza:
    - bus / bodaboda / pikipiki atakayo ituma
    - vitu vingine vya usafirishaji
    - maelezo mafupi (short note)
3.8 Seller anatuma mzigo -> buyer anapata taarifa: "nenda sehemu fulani",
    atakuta mzigo wake pamoja na maelezo madogo kutoka seller.
3.9 Mnunuzi akiona mzigo -> anathibitisha (confirm receipt).
3.10 Hapo ndipo RISITI inatoka kwa seller NA buyer.
3.11 Taarifa zote zina rekodiwa kwenye ACCOUNT STATEMENT (sio seller
    statement tu) na details zote (2026 mwaka).

=====================================================================
[4] NOTIFICATIONS - mfumo mzima
=====================================================================
4.1 Kila situation -> notification inaingia kwenye SCREEN ya notification.
4.2 Push notification = OneSignal.
4.3 Heads-up notification = LOCAL notification (iko kwenye device).
4.4 SMS (meseji) inatumwa TU kwa:
    - malipo yamekamilika
    - malipo hayajakamilika
    - ku-send OTP
4.5 Notification zisionekane MARA MBILI (dedup) - hususani order details.

=====================================================================
[5] UTENGANO WA SEHEMU (kila kipengele kazi yake)
=====================================================================
5.1 Sehemu ya kuangalia order -> kazi yake ni kuangalia order TU.
5.2 Sehemu ya kuweka shipping fee -> kazi yake ni hiyo TU.
5.3 Sehemu ya kutuma bidhaa -> kazi yake ni hiyo TU.
5.4 Sehemu ya kuweka address -> kazi yake ni hiyo TU.
5.5 Hakuna kipengele kinachoingilia kazi ya kingine.

=====================================================================
[6] ADDRESS / MIKOA YA TANZANIA
=====================================================================

6.2 Kwa urahisi wa selection (buyer) na seller kujua buyer yupo wapi.

[7] CHAT
=====================================================================
7.1 Chat list: onyesha JINA la member, sio ID.
7.2 Push + heads-up notification ya UNREAD BUBBLE:
    - inaonekana kwa mtumiaji ANAYEPOKEA ujumbe (sio mtumaji)
    - ikiwa hayupo kwenye chat room.
7.3 Kwenye chat room kuwe na indicator za: TYPING, OFFLINE, ONLINE.
7.4 Profile picture:
    - kama haja set picha -> icon ya mtu (person icon)
    - akiweka picha -> picha yake ionekane.
